Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you get here for a breakdown?

We aim to reach Stoke-on-Trent and North Staffordshire homes as soon as possible, often the same day. Send an enquiry and we'll confirm a time straight away.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

We tell you the cost before any work begins. You approve the price first, so nothing is added afterwards.

How long does a boiler service take?

A full service usually takes under an hour. We check safety devices, clean where needed, and confirm the boiler is running efficiently.

Can you work in tenanted properties?

Yes. We handle servicing and repairs for landlords across the area and supply the records you need for your paperwork.

How long does a bathroom installation take?

Most bathrooms are completed within one to two weeks depending on the fit-out. We give you a schedule with your fixed quote before starting.

Do you offer emergency repairs?

We prioritise households with no heating or hot water. Send an enquiry describing the fault and we'll respond urgently.
